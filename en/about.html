Company

Before the AI, there is a person.

ShinAI did not begin from an interest in technology. It began from a question: how does a person's work get passed on properly?

Purpose

Use AI to give people their human hours back.

What lies beyond efficiency is not more work. It is people returning to what only people can do.

Our aim is not to optimise operations. When AI carries information processing and routine judgment, people can concentrate on the higher work of creating, deciding, and building relationships. Our technology is pointed at supporting that kind of change in a company.

Message

A message from the founder

"I wanted to save people in trouble."
That single wish kept me on the front line as a firefighter for some seven years.

Facing lives day after day, I also came to know the limits of what one person can do. Was there a way to help far more people? I carried that question with me for years.

The answer was AI. When I encountered generative AI in February 2023, I became certain it could let me help many more people — not as a technology that replaces people, but as a means to widen what they are capable of.

What we take on is rebuilding the very workings of a company with AI at its foundation. Not efficiency, but optimisation — that is where the essence lies.

Technology is never the goal, only the means. Beyond it lies a future where people reclaim their human time and give themselves to value of a higher order. "Believe in true value, and build a new future for the next generation." That is the path we keep walking.

Future Vision
