How it works

How the free consultation goes

Online, around 30 to 45 minutes. No preparation or documents needed.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Use the form below to tell us briefly what is troubling you. We will arrange a time that works for you and send you a Zoom or similar meeting link.

  2. 02

    Talk it through online

    For 30 to 45 minutes we listen to your current work and its difficulties. No jargon, starting from what you are trying to achieve.

  3. 03

    Where to apply it, and how

    We tell you where we think you could begin and how it would proceed.
