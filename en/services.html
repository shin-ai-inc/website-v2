Solutions

Try it, deepen it, then run it yourselves.

Start with a small proof, embed the knowledge deeply, and finish with something your own team can run. ShinAI roots AI in your organisation across three stages. Beyond them, we research physical AI — the skill on a floor that documents never captured.

What We Offer

Three kinds of value we deliver

We divide our work by the outcome it produces. Begin wherever sits closest to the problem you actually have.

Unlocking tacit knowledge

A veteran's instinct and criteria for judgment live only in their head, and they leave when that person retires. We convert conversations, documents and work records into a form you can search, teach from, and lean on when deciding. "Only one person knows that" becomes knowledge anyone in the organisation can draw on.

  • Collecting and structuring your internal knowledge
  • RAG systems that put your internal data to work
  • LLM optimisation and tuning

Private AI agent development

General-purpose AI does not know your context, so it goes unused on the floor and quietly becomes a formality. We connect your internal knowledge to your existing systems and build a private agent that not only answers but carries the work through. Hand it enquiries and routine tasks, and people are free to concentrate on judgment and conversation.

  • Answers and decision support grounded in your own knowledge
  • Integration with existing systems and APIs
  • Support from search all the way to execution

In-house AI enablement

We do not treat go-live as the end of the job. We stay alongside you through PoC design, accuracy evaluation, operating rules, and growing your internal team. In time you will run and improve it without us. We hand over the technology and let it take root.

  • PoC design and evaluation design
  • Operating rules and guidelines
  • Team development and technical handover

Physical AI New service

On-site skill, into AI.

How a hand moves, how much pressure to apply, what a glance decides. We capture the on-site skill that lives in no document — through sensors and video — and put it into a form AI can work with.

A new service that extends tacit-knowledge AI into the physical floor. We start from a small trial and design it around your site.

ShinAI Method

How knowledge starts moving

Gather what is scattered, connect it by meaning, give it grounding, put it to work. Five stages, and at the end it moves in-house.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Collect

    Gather conversations, documents and work history, bringing scattered knowledge into one place.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Organise

    Sort the knowledge, the conditions behind decisions, and the relationships, then connect them by meaning.

  3. 03

    Ground

    Give evidence

    Connect to RAG so sources can be traced, and every answer carries something behind it.

  4. 04

    Act

    Execute

    Agents support search, judgment, and the work itself.

  5. 05

    Transfer

    Hand over

    Pass it to a team that can operate and improve it from the inside.

How We Work

Small trial to production, then over to you

We do not build big at the start. We move on as the effect is confirmed. The durations below are guides only and vary with scale and purpose.

  1. 1 Framing the problem and requirements approx. 1–2 weeks
  2. 2 Working prototype approx. 2–4 weeks
  3. 3 Validation on real operational data approx. 2–6 weeks
  4. 4 Production build approx. 1–3 months or more
  5. 5 In-house capability and ongoing improvement ongoing

These durations are estimates. We look at the result of each stage together and decide whether to go on.
