Industries

Every industry holds knowledge no one can see.

Knowledge staying invisible is the one problem every industry shares. Here is how that knowledge becomes visible and usable in each kind of workplace.

Use Cases

How it applies, by industry

The same problem shows up differently in each sector. We pair the difficulty you will recognise with what changes once tacit knowledge is addressed.

Manufacturing

Expert judgment, turned into a standard anyone can use

What you will recognise

The knack for inspection and adjustment is never put into words, so quality varies and the craft passes only through a few veterans.

What changes

For example, an AI that estimates quotes from drawings and past project data. Inspection records and the knack behind decisions become structured too, so newer staff judge by the same standard.

Construction

Procedures asleep on a shelf, pulled out on site

What you will recognise

Safety know-how and on-site response depend on individual experience, while the manual stays on the shelf unread.

What changes

For example, an AI that drafts daily site reports from photos and notes. Past incidents and procedures also surface through conversation, so the information you need is one question away.

Care and professional services

Nuance that lives only in people, turned into knowledge

What you will recognise

A veteran's care decisions and the nuance of client relationships never reach the records, and leave with them when they retire.

What changes

For example, an AI that drafts handover notes and assessments from care records. Daily records and conversations also become knowledge, answering questions of judgment. The load comes down.

Retail

Each store's instinct, made a shared strength

What you will recognise

A feel for service and assortment stays sealed inside each store, and enquiries eat into the day.

What changes

For example, an AI that connects your CRM data with service records. Product knowledge and service history come together into a guide that supports both staff questions and customer response.

Healthcare and welfare

Scattered information, gathered with its sources

What you will recognise

Specialist knowledge and past cases sit scattered, and the hours dissolve into simply looking for what you need.

What changes

For example, an AI that eliminates manual entry from charts and referral letters. Internal documents and case records also become searchable with their sources attached, cutting double-checking and transcription errors.

Education

One teacher's craft, everyone's asset

What you will recognise

The craft of teaching and building materials stays with the individual, so quality varies between classes and training sessions.

What changes

For example, an AI that suggests student-specific learning advice from past teaching records. Materials and know-how also become a shared asset that underpins learning support shaped to each student.

Finance

Grounds for a decision, put into explainable form

What you will recognise

The reasoning behind assessments and client advice goes unspoken, making accountability and handover a weight to carry.

What changes

For example, an underwriting support AI that cites criteria and past approvals with their grounds, supporting the work in a form you can explain. The final call stays with a person.

Real estate

Scattered detail, searched across the whole

What you will recognise

Property detail and a feel for negotiation sit apart in each agent's head, so enquiries stall.

What changes

For example, an AI that drafts a first pass of the key-facts disclosure from property documents. Contact history also searches alongside it, so answers and proposals come together quickly.

Beyond Industry

Different industries, the same root problem.

Knowledge stuck with one person, uncertainty about where to start, worry over connecting existing systems. The shape differs by industry, but the root problem is always the same.

  • Tacit knowledge stuck with one person

    Years of instinct and criteria live only in one head, and no handover document reproduces them.

  • Not sure where to start

    You feel the problem, but can't tell which part of the operation to structure first, so nothing moves.

  • Worried about connecting existing systems

    Your data already lives in a CRM or core system, and it's unclear whether AI can connect without replacing it.

ShinAI works from your current operations and knowledge assets to find the shape that fits your company.
