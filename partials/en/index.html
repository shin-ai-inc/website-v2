Physical AI — R&D

The skill that never became words stays in the hands.

Documents and conversations are within reach of AI now. But the way a veteran's hand moves, how much pressure they apply, what they decide from a glance — none of that was ever written down. That is what we are researching.

Motion and task intelligence

Capturing how skilled workers move, through sensors and video, and using reinforcement learning to make it reproducible. Skill that never fit in a manual, turned into knowledge a machine can work with.

Sensing and judgment on site

The sound of a machine running, vibration, temperature, images. We are testing systems that read the continuous signals coming off the real world and catch early signs of failure or drift in quality sooner than a person can.

Running on the edge

Putting AI on the equipment itself, so it keeps working where the network does not. We are trialling designs that process data on the spot when it cannot be sent to the cloud.

Physical AI is at the research and proof-of-concept stage. We do not present it as a finished product — we develop it jointly with companies that have a real site to test on. We are looking for joint research and pilot partners.
